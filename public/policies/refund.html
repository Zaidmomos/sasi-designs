Returns, Exchange & Refund Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

7-day size exchange

If the size doesn't fit, you can ask for an exchange within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed and have their original tags.

Damaged or wrong items

If you receive a damaged, defective or wrong item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We'll replace it or refund you in full, including shipping.

Items we can't take back

Items that have been worn, washed or altered, and items bought on final sale, can't be returned or exchanged.

Refunds

Where a refund is approved, online payments are refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 working days. For cash on delivery orders, we refund by UPI or bank transfer to an account you give us.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order before it ships by contacting us. Prepaid orders cancelled before shipping are refunded in full.

How to request

Message us on WhatsApp or email us with your order number (for example SD-1001). See our contact page.
