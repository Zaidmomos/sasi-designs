Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data Sasi Designs collects, why, and how we protect it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, mobile number, delivery address, city, PIN code and, if you give it, your email address and any note you add. We also keep a record of what you ordered and how you paid.

What we don't collect

We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Online payments are processed by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway, under its own privacy policy.

Why we use it

Only to process and deliver your order, contact you about it (by phone, WhatsApp or email), handle returns and refunds, and keep the records the law requires. We don't sell your data or send marketing messages without your consent.

Who we share it with

Our courier partners (to deliver your order), Razorpay (to take payment) and our secure hosting and database providers. Each receives only what it needs.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as tax and accounting laws require. You can ask us to delete other personal data at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by contacting us. We'll respond within 30 days.
