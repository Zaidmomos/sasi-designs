Shipping Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Where we ship

We deliver to all serviceable PIN codes in India. We don't ship internationally yet.

Processing time

Orders are packed and handed to our courier partner within 2–3 working days (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays).

Delivery time

Most orders arrive within 3–7 working days of dispatch. Remote areas may take longer.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free on orders above the amount shown on our website. A flat shipping fee applies to smaller orders; it is shown in your bag before you pay.

Tracking

Once your order ships, we'll send you the courier name and tracking number on WhatsApp.

Cash on delivery

For cash on delivery orders, please keep the exact amount ready. Orders refused at delivery without a valid reason may make you ineligible for cash on delivery in future.
